An uncharted island, and the rocks nobody drew

Fifty-four landings this app owes a person. Take the handset, make each one, and mark what you find. What you cannot reach goes on the chart as a hazard.

Why a survey and not a test run. Two release-relevant defects turned up in one evening, and every automated instrument we own was blind to both.

The unread badge sat six pixels from the mute bell, so ▼ general (3) 🔕 read as one compound signal meaning the opposite of what it said — composed at AppBar.title across two files that never appear in a single diff.

And long-pressing a row in the conversation dropdown does nothing: no handler was ever wired. showConversationMuteMenu has exactly one caller, while the comment claiming the dropdown rows "call this directly instead" describes something that was never built.

Route coverage proves everything built is reachable. It cannot see either of these. The first is geometry; the second has no route to be unreachable. A capability nobody built has nothing to go red. That is the water this chart covers.

    What the survey should have found, as of 0.0.4 on 1 September 2026 — with rows 20, 33 and 34 revised on 5 September when task #26 landed.

    Deliberately absent. Calling is gated off entirely — three doors shut (the long-press sheet, the ring banner, and the /call/:channelId route, which was never registered). Media crosses the island SFU in the clear with nothing disclosing it, and CallKit is designed-not-built, so an invite to a backgrounded app is silence. Taking back a sent message is also absent: retractions are consumed from the wire, but nothing app-side originates one.

    When this book was first written, every one of those but the last two was a long-press: the whole moderation and attention surface hid behind an invisible gesture, on the platform where there is no right-click to fall back on. That pattern was the finding, not any single row of it. Rows 20, 33 and 34 have since been rebuilt — the title means this conversation and opens details where mute lives, and the conversation list moved to a drawer (task #26). The rest still hide.

    This chart is hand-drawn, so it is exactly as complete as whoever drew it. It will not find a capability nobody thought to name — the same blindness it exists to catch, one level up. Add a landing whenever a real person wants something that is not here. That, and not a passing test run, is what keeps it honest.
