You cannot serialize an un-cancellable round trip without inheriting its hang, and you cannot timeout that hang without issuing a second write — which is defect five again. 3, 6, 9: guard 3 was the wait, this is 6 wearing a loop, the island's unconditional 204 is 9 — the lie that lets `believed = desired` on a no-op.

**If RECAST, what to fold back:**
- **Demote the uniqueness claim** in "The invariant" to: this *isolate* is the only *live* writer; already-on-the-wire writes from a dead incarnation and backup-restored debts are named residuals. Same-process re-login must not issue HTTP of its own.
